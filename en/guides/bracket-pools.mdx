---
title: "Bracket Pools: Predict the Full Knockout Draw"
description: "How Cascading Bracket mode works in Toqui: submit your full knockout bracket before R16, earn escalating points, and watch upsets ripple through standings."
---

A bracket pool is a prediction pool where you commit to the entire knockout stage all at once — before a single match is played. You pick who wins every Round of 16 matchup, who advances to the quarterfinals, the semis, and ultimately who lifts the trophy. Once the bracket locks, you're along for the ride.

This is Toqui's **Cascading Bracket** mode. It's the most dramatic way to run a pool. It's also the hardest.

<Note>
  Cascading Bracket is one of three preset scoring modes. To compare it with Casual and Escalating, see [Scoring modes](/en/guides/scoring-modes).
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your pool uses Cascading Bracket mode">
    The organizer selects Cascading Bracket when creating the pool. This enables the bracket prediction interface for all members.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Members submit their picks as matchups open">
    Each knockout matchup opens for predictions as soon as both teams in it are confirmed — usually right after the group-stage matches that determine those teams finish. So picks for the first knockout round become available during the group stage; quarterfinal picks open as the first knockout round plays out; and so on.

    Members pick a winner for every knockout match across the bracket — first knockout round, quarterfinals, semifinals, third-place match, and final. You don't have to enter them all in one session — come back as new matchups open and adjust any prediction up until the bracket-wide lock.

    You're not predicting scores at this stage, just outcomes: which team advances from each matchup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The bracket locks">
    By default, once the first knockout match kicks off, picks lock for everyone — including any matchups whose teams aren't yet confirmed (those simply remain unfilled). One deadline for the entire bracket: the first knockout-round kickoff (Round of 32 or Round of 16, depending on the tournament), with no per-match or per-round deadlines. Organizers who want a different cadence can switch knockout stages to per-match locks on the [Scoring Rules screen](/en/guides/create-pool).

    <Warning>
      There's no grace period once the bracket locks. Set a reminder before the first knockout match starts so you don't miss the deadline.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Teams advance — or crash out">
    As matches are played, the app tracks which teams actually advance. If you predicted a team to win their first knockout match and they do, you earn points and they "auto-advance" in your bracket — they're slotted into your predicted quarterfinal matchup.

    If your predicted team loses, you earn no points for that match, and every subsequent prediction that depended on that team is also lost. An early upset can unravel an otherwise strong bracket.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Points accumulate through the final">
    Points scale with each stage. Getting the final right is worth far more than getting a Round of 16 match right. A member who predicts a lot of early upsets can be overtaken in the last two rounds by someone who called the final correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How points work

Points use the same escalating scale as the Escalating preset. Correct picks in later rounds earn significantly more than early rounds.

| Stage | Exact score | Correct outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Round of 16 | 7 pts | 5 pts |
| Quarter-final | 10 pts | 7 pts |
| Semi-final | 15 pts | 10 pts |
| Third-place match | 20 pts | 15 pts |
| Final | 30 pts | 20 pts |

"Exact score" in a bracket pool means you also predicted the exact scoreline for that match (optional — the bracket interface lets you add score predictions on top of your outcome picks).

Picking who advances doesn't earn points by itself — the value of getting a matchup right is that the team you picked stays alive in your bracket for the *next* prediction. If the matchup you predicted for a specific match never happens (because one of the teams was knocked out earlier), you earn **0 points for that match regardless of the scoreline you wrote down**.

As in every other mode, scoring uses the **full-time + extra time** result. Penalty kicks don't factor into the calculation — see [How scores are read](/en/guides/scoring-modes#how-scores-are-read).

## The cascade effect

The name "Cascading Bracket" refers to what happens when an upset occurs. Your predicted bracket assumed Team A would reach the final. If Team A is knocked out in the quarters, all the points you had lined up for the semis and final — for every match you expected Team A to play — disappear with them.

This creates moments of collective suspense that a regular per-match pool doesn't produce. One result can simultaneously reshape dozens of members' standings.

## The deadline: don't miss it

All bracket picks must be submitted before the first knockout-round kickoff (Round of 32 or Round of 16, depending on the tournament). That's a single hard deadline for the entire bracket — even though individual matchups open progressively as teams are confirmed during the group stage.

<Tip>
  Share the deadline date and time with your group when you share the invite link. A WhatsApp reminder the day before the first knockout match starts goes a long way.
</Tip>

## Is bracket mode right for your group?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When bracket mode works best">
    Bracket mode is best for groups where members want to commit early and then largely just watch. It's low-maintenance compared to per-match modes — no per-match or per-round deadlines for the knockouts. Members fill the bracket as matchups open up, then sit back as the tournament plays out.

    It rewards people who have a strong sense of how deep teams will go in the tournament, rather than who can predict individual match scores.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When to choose Escalating instead">
    If your group includes members who want to engage with every match — submitting fresh picks each round, adjusting their strategy based on how the group stage went — Escalating gives them that. Bracket mode takes that choice away by design.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can members skip the bracket and still use the pool?">
    No. In Cascading Bracket mode, the bracket submission is the pool. Members who don't submit a bracket before the deadline won't have knockout stage picks and won't earn points for those rounds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
