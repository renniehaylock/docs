---

Your Toqui profile controls how other pool members see you on leaderboards and pick summaries. You can update your display name and pick an emoji avatar at any time — no profile photo required.

## What you can set

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Display name" icon="pen">
    The name other members see next to your picks and ranking. Change it any time from your profile settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Emoji avatar" icon="face-smile">
    Pick any emoji from the built-in set and pair it with a background color. No image uploads needed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to update your display name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Go to [toqui.app/app/profile](https://toqui.app/app/profile) or tap your avatar in the top-right corner of any page and select **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your display name">
    Click the **Display name** field and type your new name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. Your new name appears across all your pools immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to pick or change your emoji avatar

Your avatar is an emoji paired with a background color — no photo uploads, no cropping tools.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Go to [toqui.app/app/profile](https://toqui.app/app/profile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click your current avatar">
    A picker opens showing the full emoji set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an emoji">
    Tap any emoji to select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a background color">
    Pick from the color swatches below the emoji grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes** to apply your new avatar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your avatar is shared across every pool you're in. If you're a member of multiple pools, all of them show the same emoji and color.
</Note>

## Sign-in methods

Toqui supports three ways to sign in. You choose one when you create your account.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Google">
    Click **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page. Toqui uses your Google account for authentication — your Google profile photo is not imported as your Toqui avatar.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Facebook">
    Click **Continue with Facebook** on the sign-in page. Same as Google: authentication only, no photo import.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email and password">
    Enter your email address and choose a password. You can reset your password from the sign-in page at any time.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Regardless of how you sign in, your display name and emoji avatar are what other members see — not your Google or Facebook name.
</Tip>
