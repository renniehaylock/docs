## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the minimum group size for Enterprise?">
    Enterprise is designed for groups of 1,000 or more. If your pool has fewer than 1,000 members but you need whitelabel branding, contact sales anyway — we'll find the right fit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    For most setups, about one to two business days after you provide your branding assets and member list. Reach out early — pools launched well before the tournament opens tend to see better participation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can we pay by invoice instead of credit card?">
    Yes. Enterprise invoicing supports purchase orders and wire transfers. Enterprise invoices can itemize applicable taxes for accounting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this legal to run at work?">
    A recreational prediction pool with no money flowing through the platform sits in the same legal category as a March Madness bracket at the office. Toqui takes no entry fees and pays no cash prizes. Any internal buy-in your company runs is between you and your employees. Verify with your own legal or HR team before launching. This isn't legal advice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
