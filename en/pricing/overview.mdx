Toqui is free for groups up to 25 members — no credit card, no expiration, no catch. When your group grows past that, the organizer makes a one-time payment for the whole tournament. Members never pay to join a pool.

## Pro tier pricing

Pro is a one-time payment per pool, not a subscription. Pick the tier that fits your group size.

| Tier | Max members | Price (one-time) | Per person |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small Office | 50 | $125 | $2.50 |
| Medium Office | 100 | $250 | $2.50 |
| Large Office | 250 | $625 | $2.50 |
| Large Company | 500 | $1,250 | $2.50 |
| Max Pro | 1,000 | $2,500 | $2.50 |

<Note>
  If your pool grows past its tier mid-tournament, you can upgrade at any time. You pay only the difference — no picks are lost and nobody has to rejoin.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the Free plan really free?">
    Yes. No credit card, no ads, no catch. Create unlimited pools with up to 25 members each, forever. If your group never grows past 25, you never pay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do pool members have to pay?">
    Never. Joining a pool is always free — one tap on a shareable link and you're in. Only the organizer pays, and only if the pool grows past 25 members.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if my pool hits the member limit?">
    New members can't join once you hit the cap. Toqui will let you know in-app. Upgrade to the next Pro tier and pay only the difference — no picks are lost, no data is reset, and no one has to rejoin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this a subscription?">
    No. Pro is a one-time payment per pool for the entire tournament. There's no monthly charge and no renewal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Pro and Enterprise?">
    Pro is self-serve: pick your tier, start in minutes, up to 1,000 members. Enterprise is for groups of 1,000+ or companies that need whitelabel branding, a dedicated account manager, and enterprise invoicing. If you're running an office pool, Pro is almost always enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a World Cup office pool legal in the US?">
    For a recreational group activity, yes — same legal footing as a March Madness bracket at the office. Toqui takes no money in or out of the platform: no entry fees to Toqui, no cash prizes paid by Toqui. Any buy-in your group runs is off-platform and between you and your coworkers. Check your company's HR or legal policy for internal rules. This isn't legal advice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
