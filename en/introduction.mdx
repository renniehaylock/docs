Toqui is a private soccer prediction pool app for groups who want to compete on their own knowledge — not get lost in a public bracket with two million strangers. Create a pool, invite your group via a link, and let Toqui handle everything else: private picks before each match, real-time auto-scoring when the final whistle blows, and a live leaderboard that updates itself. No spreadsheets, no manual math, no commissioner chasing everyone for picks.

## Who it's for

Toqui works for any group that follows soccer together:

- **Friend groups** running a World Cup 2026 pool in the group chat
- **Offices and companies** competing department-vs-department
- **Communities and clubs** who want a self-serve pool without a subscription

If you've ever watched an Excel sheet die by Matchday 3, Toqui is the alternative.

## Pricing at a glance

Pools are free for groups up to 25 members — no credit card, no time limit. Above 25 members, only the pool organizer ever pays a one-time fee for that pool. Members always join free.

<Note>
  No money changes hands through Toqui — the platform has no cash prizes and no betting. Toqui is 100% recreational, in the same category as a March Madness bracket at work.
</Note>
