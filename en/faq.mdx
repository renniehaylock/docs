Toqui is a private soccer prediction pool app for friend groups, offices, and communities. If you're new or just have a quick question, you're likely to find the answer below. Can't find what you need? Email [help@toqui.app](mailto:help@toqui.app).

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Toqui free?">
    Yes — completely free for pools with up to 25 members. You can create as many pools as you like, and the free tier never expires and never requires a credit card. You only pay if a pool grows beyond 25 members, and even then only the organizer pays — not the members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do members have to pay to join?">
    Never. Joining a pool is always free for every participant. One tap on a shareable invite link (or entering an 8-character join code) and you're in. Only the pool organizer pays, and only when the pool grows past 25 members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to download an app?">
    No download required. Toqui is a web app — open [toqui.app](https://toqui.app) in any modern browser and you're good to go.

    If you'd like a more native feel, you can install Toqui as a Progressive Web App (PWA):

    - **iOS (Safari):** Tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
    - **Android (Chrome):** Tap the browser menu, then **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app**.
    - **Desktop (Chrome/Edge):** Click the install icon in the address bar.

    The installed PWA gives you a home-screen icon, full-screen experience, and push notifications.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What tournaments does Toqui support?">
    Toqui is built for the **World Cup 2026** (48 teams, 104 matches, June 11 – July 19, hosted across the US, Canada, and Mexico) and other major soccer tournaments including Copa América. More tournaments are added as they approach — check the tournament selector when creating a pool for the current list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Pools and picks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a public pool and a private pool?">
    - **Public pool:** Anyone with the invite link can join — no extra code needed. Useful for larger, open groups.
    - **Private pool:** Members must enter the pool's unique 8-character join code to get in. The organizer controls who gets the code.

    Both pool types keep individual picks hidden from other members until the deadline locks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I be in more than one pool at the same time?">
    Yes. You can join or create up to 50 pools simultaneously. If you try to create a pool beyond that limit, you'll see an error asking you to leave an existing pool first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my picks after submitting?">
    Yes, as long as the deadline hasn't passed. Edit your picks any time before the lock:

    - **Casual mode:** Picks lock individually at each match's kickoff.
    - **Escalating / Cascading Bracket:** Picks lock at the start of the stage (e.g., all group-stage picks lock on the day the group stage begins).

    Once a match or stage has locked, picks for that match can no longer be added or changed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I miss the pick deadline?">
    Your prediction for that match is not submitted, and you earn 0 points for it. There's no way to retroactively enter a pick after the deadline has passed. Set up push notifications so Toqui can remind you before picks lock.

    <Tip>
      Enable push notifications in your account settings to get a reminder a few hours before each deadline.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see other members' picks before the deadline?">
    No. All picks are private until the match or stage deadline locks. This prevents anyone from simply copying the picks of the person who actually watches soccer — everyone competes on their own knowledge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does 'picks lock' mean?">
    "Picks lock" is the deadline after which no new predictions can be submitted for a given match or tournament stage. Depending on your pool's scoring mode:

    - In **Casual** mode, picks lock at the kickoff time of each individual match.
    - In **Escalating** or **Cascading Bracket** mode, all picks for a stage lock together at the start of that stage.

    After a lock, submitted picks become visible to all pool members.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Scoring

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does scoring work?">
    Scoring depends on the mode your organizer chose when creating the pool. Toqui awards points for different levels of prediction accuracy:

    | Rule | What it rewards | Default points |
    |------|----------------|---------------|
    | Correct outcome | Right result — home win, draw, or away win | 2 |
    | Exact score | The exact final scoreline | 5 |
    | Correct margin | Right goal difference | 3 |
    | Total goals | Right total number of goals | 2 |
    | Clean sheet | Correctly predicted a team conceding 0 goals | 2 |
    | Close score | Each predicted score within 1 goal of the actual score | 1 |

    Organizers can customize which rules apply and adjust the point values per tournament stage. In **Escalating** and **Cascading Bracket** modes, point values increase as the tournament progresses — a correct outcome in the final is worth far more than one in the group stage.

    <Note>
      Your organizer may have changed the default point values. Check your pool's scoring rules page to see exactly what's in play for your pool.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When does the leaderboard update?">
    Within seconds of a match ending. Toqui receives live match data and updates scores and standings automatically — no organizer action needed, no page refresh required. You may receive a push notification if you've moved up the table.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are tiebreakers handled?">
    When two or more members have the same total points, Toqui resolves ties in this order:

    1. **Total points** — higher is better (the primary sort).
    2. **Number of exact scores** — more exact-score hits wins the tiebreak.
    3. **Goal-difference accuracy** — the member whose predicted goal differences are closest to reality across all matches ranks higher.
    4. **Average submission time** — the member who submitted their picks earlier on average ranks higher.

    These tiers ensure a tiebreak can almost always be resolved without a manual ruling.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Pricing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a World Cup office pool legal in the US?">
    For a recreational group activity, yes — a World Cup prediction pool sits on the same legal footing as a March Madness bracket at the office. Toqui takes no money in or out of the platform: there are no entry fees paid to us and no cash prizes paid by us. Any buy-in your group runs among yourselves lives entirely off-platform and between your group members.

    <Warning>
      Check your company's HR or legal policy before running a pool at work — some employers have rules about workplace pools regardless of the platform. This is not legal advice.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my pool hits the member limit?">
    Once a pool reaches its tier's member cap, new members can't join. Toqui will notify the organizer in-app when the limit is reached. The organizer can upgrade to the next Pro tier at any time — paying only the difference in price — and no picks or data are lost. Existing members don't need to rejoin.

    Pro tier caps: **50, 100, 250, 500, and 1,000 members**. Pools with over 1,000 members require an Enterprise plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    For billing questions, refund requests, or any payment issues, email [help@toqui.app](mailto:help@toqui.app). Include your pool name and the email address on your account and the team will get back to you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Technical

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What browsers does Toqui support?">
    Toqui works in all modern browsers:

    - **Chrome** (desktop and Android) — full support including PWA install and push notifications.
    - **Firefox** — full support.
    - **Safari** (macOS and iOS) — full support. PWA push notifications on iOS require **Safari 16.4 or later** and the app must be installed to your Home Screen first.
    - **Edge** — full support.

    Very old browser versions (Internet Explorer, Safari below 14) are not supported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I install Toqui as an app on my phone or desktop?">
    Toqui is installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA), which gives you a home-screen icon, full-screen experience, and push notifications without going through an app store.

    **iPhone / iPad (Safari required):**
    1. Open [toqui.app](https://toqui.app) in Safari.
    2. Tap the **Share** button (the box with an arrow pointing up).
    3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
    4. Tap **Add** to confirm.

    **Android (Chrome):**
    1. Open [toqui.app](https://toqui.app) in Chrome.
    2. Tap the three-dot menu.
    3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app**.

    **Desktop (Chrome or Edge):**
    1. Open [toqui.app](https://toqui.app).
    2. Click the install icon (a monitor with a download arrow) in the browser's address bar.
    3. Click **Install** in the confirmation prompt.

    <Note>
      On iOS, push notifications only work when Toqui is installed as a PWA via Safari and your iOS version is 16.4 or later.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
