## How Toqui works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a pool">
    Sign up, pick a tournament (World Cup 2026, Copa América, and more), choose a scoring mode, and name your pool. Takes about 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your group">
    Share a one-tap invite link in your group chat or text your join code. Members join for free — no payment required to participate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Everyone submits picks">
    Each member enters their predicted score for every match. Picks stay private until the deadline locks — no copying the person who actually watches soccer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toqui scores everything automatically">
    The moment the final whistle blows, Toqui updates every score and refreshes the leaderboard. Smart notifications let members know when they move up the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>
